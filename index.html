About

Finance advice that holds up in the real world

I am a Chartered Accountant working across taxation, audit and business advisory. My practice covers the full statutory cycle — income tax and GST compliance, internal audit and assurance — alongside the advisory work that sits on top of it: structuring a new venture, tightening internal controls, and helping management teams actually read their own numbers.

I trained at Marathe Padhye & Athalye in Pune, where I represented clients before the tax authorities, ran internal and statutory audits, and built the budgeting and MIS discipline that still shapes how I work. I then served as Head of Finance at COPA-DATA India, which put me on the client's side of the table as well as the adviser's.

That combination is why the Virtual CFO engagement is the centre of my practice today: businesses rarely need only a filing done — they need someone who can own the finance function end to end without carrying a full-time CFO on the payroll.

Virtual CFO

  • Short-, medium- and long-term planning
  • SWOT analysis
  • Support on organisational structure

02 Policies & Procedures

  • Fund management
  • HR & manpower
  • IT controls and mechanisms
  • Trade policies — dealerships and agencies
  • Designing critical SOPs
  • Forex management

03 Finance & Accounts

  • Monthly, quarterly and half-yearly review of financials
  • Operational and departmental MIS review
  • Cash-flow management support
  • Budgeting and monitoring
  • Banking and institutional management
  • Inventory control
  • Assessment of legal, commercial, accounting and tax matters
  • Defining roles for consultants and the finance team’s KRAs
  • Compliance management and critical ratio analysis

04 Transaction Support

  • Restructuring support
  • Due diligence support
