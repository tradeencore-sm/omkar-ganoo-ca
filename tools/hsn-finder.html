HSN & SAC Code Finder

Search by code or description for the GST rate under GST 2.0 — the Nil, 5%, 18% and 40% structure in force since 22 September 2025, when the 12% and 28% slabs were withdrawn.

    Indicative reference only. Classification under the Customs Tariff drives the rate, and many entries turn on packaging, the value of the item, or the end use — the conditions noted against a row are summaries, not the full entry. Rates also change by notification. Confirm the exact rate on the GST portal's HSN/SAC search or the CBIC rate finder before you raise an invoice. This page is not tax advice.
